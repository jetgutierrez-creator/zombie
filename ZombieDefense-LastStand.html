OBJECTIVE

Zombies pour in from the ruins every wave. Keep them off the barricades — once a barricade falls they hit the base core directly. If base integrity reaches zero, it's over.

TIPS

  • Repair barricades with E between waves — 40 coins each.
  • Bloaters detonate. Kill them at range, never in the barricade line.
  • Brutes soak damage — the shotgun up close shreds them.
  • Coins persist between runs. Die, upgrade, come back stronger.
  • Every 10th wave spawns an Abomination. Keep moving.
  • Barricades are fully repaired free every 5 waves.
